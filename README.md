What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.
Salesforce CRM systems help companies manage:
Customers
Sales
Support cases
Marketing
Business relationships

A CRM stores customer information in one place so teams can track interactions and improve customer service and sales.

Example

If a customer:

visits a website,
buys a product,
raises a support ticket,

the CRM records all these activities.
